Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

HomeTerms of Service

GTA 6 Clip Bot (the "App") is a personal tool run by one individual (the "Operator") on their own computer. It has no public users, no sign-up, and no website that collects information. This page explains what the App does with data.

1. Information the App handles

  • The Operator's account credentials for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. When the Operator connects their own accounts, the platforms issue access tokens. These are stored only in a configuration file on the Operator's computer and are used only to upload videos that the Operator has approved.
  • Public Twitch stream video and chat activity. To find good moments, the App records public live streams temporarily and reads public chat. For chat it counts how fast messages arrive and checks for reaction words. It does not save the text of chat messages or the names of chatters.
  • Streamer speech. Audio from a clip is turned into captions by speech-to-text software that runs locally on the Operator's computer. The audio is not sent to any outside service.
  • Clip records. The App keeps a local list of clips with the streamer's public Twitch channel name, the reason the clip was chosen, the caption, and the posting result.

2. What the App does not do

The App does not collect personal information from visitors, viewers, or followers. It does not read TikTok, Instagram or Facebook users' private data, direct messages, contacts, or follower lists. It does not sell, rent, or share information, and it uses no advertising or tracking tools.

3. Where data is kept and for how long

Everything is stored on the Operator's own computer. Temporary stream recordings are deleted automatically within minutes. Finished clips and the clip list stay until the Operator deletes them. Tokens remain until the Operator disconnects the app or revokes access.

4. Data shared with other services

The App sends data only to the platforms the Operator chooses to publish to: an approved video and its caption go to TikTok, Instagram and Facebook through their official APIs. Those platforms handle the content under their own privacy policies. The App also contacts Twitch to read public stream and clip information.

5. Revoking access

The Operator can disconnect the App at any time from the app settings of their TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts, which invalidates its tokens. Anyone who appears in a clip and wants it removed can use the contact below.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
