GTA 6 Clip Bot

A personal tool for making and publishing short gameplay clips.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

GTA 6 Clip Bot is a small personal application run by one individual on their own computer. It finds funny and exciting moments from public Twitch streams of Grand Theft Auto VI, edits them into short vertical videos with captions and a credit to the streamer, and holds each video for the owner's manual review. After the owner approves a video, the app uploads it to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts.

The app has a single user, its owner. It does not offer an account system, a public service, or any way for other people to sign in or submit data.

Questions or requests, including content removal requests from streamers, can be sent through GitHub Issues.

Operated by an individual (GitHub: snakejdc).
